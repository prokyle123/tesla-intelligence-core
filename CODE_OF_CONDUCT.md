# Code of Conduct

Tesla Intelligence Core is an open-source enthusiast project. Participation should be useful, technical and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful toward other contributors and users.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith while still asking for reproducible evidence.
- Keep compatibility reports factual.
- Respect privacy when sharing vehicle telemetry.
- Redact credentials, VINs, exact locations and other sensitive information.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats or targeted abuse.
- Do not intentionally disrupt project spaces or support threads.

## Technical disagreements

Disagreement is normal.

When possible, support claims with:

- code;
- logs;
- measurements;
- reproducible steps;
- documented provider behavior;
- redacted telemetry examples.

The goal is to make the software better, not to win an argument.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its community.
